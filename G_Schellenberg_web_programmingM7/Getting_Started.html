Getting Started

  • Purchasing your first Airsoft Gun

    Many people get confused on what airsoft gun they should start with, mainly because there are the catagory of weapon like rifles or shotguns. Firstly if your starting airsoft the best weapon to start with is an AEG or Automatic-Electric-Gun, because its cheaper than using co2 or green gas to power your weapons, and is stronger than spring powered and is normally made with stronger materials so its more durable. For the type of weapon, most airsofters will recommend buying a rifle because rifles are well rounded and most of the time amazing out of the box. They are also a good start to find out how you like playing. We would reccomend the "lancer tactical lt-02c m4 cqbr aeg", and you can purchase extra mags so you dont run out mid game.

  • Other Necessities

    Your mask

    Your going to need a mask to protect your face from bbs, if you played paintball your mask would work if you dont then your going to need to spend around 20-50 bucks for a mask to protect your eyes and teeth. Also be careful buying masks that dont say how much impact resistance it has. Because for an airsoft mask to be considered safe, it should be rated for impact resistance, ideally with a Z87+ standard. This means it should be able to withstand a 0.2g airsoft BB impact at speeds of at least 600 fps, altho some masks may have a higher impact resistance.

  • BB Loader & bbs

    One of the many thing you'll probably need is a speedloader, this allow you to fill your mags faster hense the name "speed"loader. Now there are 2 different types of speed loaders that i am going to be talking about the old and the new ones. The old speedloaders use a stick and spring that puts about 5 bbs per push and are cheaper. Then there are the new ones that attach to the mag and you use a crank to constantly put bbs into your mag, this is normally easier to use but are more expensive. And of course you need BBs for your guns, average bbs are .20 grams which it provides a good balance of factors, including speed, accuracy, and cost. There are heavier bbs that enhance range and accuracy, especially in outdoor or long-range scenarios. Heavier BBs are less affected by wind, allowing them to travel straighter and further but use stronger guns, which may limit what fields you can go to because of fps limits. You should look at the gun you have so you dont put lighter bbs because it may shatter the bb and damage the barrel of your gun along with anyone using mesh masks altho that rarely happens. If you want your airsoft gun to last a long time then don't EVER use .12 gram bb's. The reason being is that .12 gram bb's always break in your gun, especially if you have a CO2 pistol or a high quality sniper or assault rifle, and could injure anyone who doesnt have a full face mask. And the .12 bbs are very low quality so it wont go far and is normally very inacurate.

  • Stuff your Probably already have

    Normally you should use a pair of jeans and a long sleve shirt when you go airsofting so you dont fell the pain as much. Now some airsofters have an entire ghillie suit for airsoft while others will show up in onesies and other funny stuff. You should bring some water with you because even short games will make you tired and thirsty, and being hydrated is important for obvious reasons and you often will get thirsty multiple times a day.
